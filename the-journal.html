The Rising Journal

A 90-Day Practice

A Daily Companion for Rebuilding, Rising, Becoming.

The Rising Journal is not a diary. It is a structured daily practice — designed for women who are rebuilding, and who want their reflection work to feel as intentional as the life they are creating.

Ninety days. Three phases. One becoming.

Phase One — Attract (Days 1–30). Building belief. Making space for hope, vision, and the courage to want more.

Phase Two — Align (Days 31–60). Returning to yourself. Clarity, purpose, and the deeper work of healing.

Phase Three — Empower (Days 61–90). Moving forward. Confidence, action, and owning the chapter you are writing.

What’s Inside

Built for the woman who is serious about her next chapter.

Each morning

Intention Prompt

A single focused question to open the day with purpose, not pressure.

Each day

Daily Affirmation

A centered italic statement — designed to be read before you write.

Each evening

Evening Reflection

Space to process, release, and track how far you’ve come.

Three times

Pillar Dividers

Each phase opens with a reflection on the pillar ahead — Attract, Align, Empower.

Throughout

Gratitude & Wins

A quiet record of progress — because rebuilding deserves to be witnessed.
