Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to your most common questions.

Coaching and therapy are different but complementary. Therapy often focuses on healing the past and diagnosing mental health conditions, while coaching is forward-focused — helping you build the life you want from where you are now. If you're in active mental health crisis, I will always recommend you work with a licensed therapist.

Many clients notice meaningful shifts within the first few sessions — a new perspective, a decision they'd been avoiding, or simply a deep sense of relief. Lasting transformation unfolds over weeks and months, which is why I offer packages rather than one-off sessions. Your commitment to the process is the biggest factor.
