From Survival to Purpose

There was a time I thought the weight of what I was carrying would never lift. I wore a smile for the world while inside I was quietly falling apart — navigating loss, identity, and a life that no longer felt like mine.

But in that silence — the stillness after the storm — I heard something I had drowned out for years: my own voice. It was soft at first, almost a whisper. Over time, it grew into a conviction. I was not broken. I was becoming.

That journey — from barely surviving to truly thriving — is the foundation of everything I do. I became a certified coach, immersed myself in healing modalities, and built a practice dedicated to one thing: helping women find that same voice within themselves.

Whether you're navigating a life transition, healing from heartbreak, building the courage to finally bet on yourself — I am here, walking alongside you with lived experience, professional training, and unwavering belief in your potential.
